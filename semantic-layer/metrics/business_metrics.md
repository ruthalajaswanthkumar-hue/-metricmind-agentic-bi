# Business Metrics

## Revenue

Definition:
Total money generated from sales.

Formula:

Revenue = SUM(sales_amount)

Database Column:

sales_amount

Business Meaning:

Shows total business income.

---

## Profit

Definition:

Money remaining after expenses.

Formula:

Profit = Revenue − Cost

Database Column:

profit

Business Meaning:

Shows business earnings.

---

## Orders

Definition:

Total number of orders.

Formula:

COUNT(order_id)

Database Column:

order_id

Business Meaning:

Shows total sales transactions.

---

## Customers

Definition:

Total customers.

Formula:

COUNT(customer_id)

Database Column:

customer_id

Business Meaning:

Shows customer base.

---

## Average Order Value

Formula:

Revenue / Orders

---

## Sales

Definition:

Total sales amount.

---

## Discount

Definition:

Total discount provided.

---

## Cost

Definition:

Business expenses.

---

## Margin

Formula:

Profit / Revenue ×100

---

## Growth %

Formula:

(Current−Previous)/Previous ×100
