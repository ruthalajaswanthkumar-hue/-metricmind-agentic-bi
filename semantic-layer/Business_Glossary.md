# Business Glossary

## Objective

The purpose of this Business Glossary is to establish a standardized set of business terms that can be understood consistently by all stakeholders, including business users, analysts, developers, and decision-makers. By maintaining common definitions, organizations can reduce ambiguity, improve communication, and ensure that reports, dashboards, and business metrics are interpreted uniformly across departments.

---

# 1. Revenue

## Definition

Revenue is the total amount of income generated by a business from selling its products or services before deducting any expenses.

## Business Meaning

Revenue is considered the starting point of a company's financial performance. It represents the total money flowing into the business through its normal operations. Business managers monitor revenue to evaluate sales performance, understand market demand, and measure business growth over different periods. Although higher revenue usually indicates better sales, it does not necessarily mean the business is profitable because operational expenses, taxes, and production costs are deducted later.

## Example

A company sells 100 laptops at ₹50,000 each.

Revenue = 100 × ₹50,000 = ₹50,00,000

## Usage

Revenue is commonly used in:

- Financial Statements
- Sales Dashboards
- Business Intelligence Reports
- Executive KPI Dashboards
- Growth Analysis
- Profit Calculations

---

# 2. Profit

## Definition

Profit is the amount of money remaining after subtracting all business expenses from the total revenue.

## Business Meaning

Profit reflects the actual financial gain earned by a company after paying all operating costs, production expenses, employee salaries, taxes, rent, utilities, and other expenditures. It is one of the most important indicators of business success because it shows whether the organization is earning more money than it spends. Investors, managers, and stakeholders rely on profit to evaluate the financial health and long-term sustainability of the business.

## Example

Revenue = ₹50,00,000

Expenses = ₹38,00,000

Profit = ₹12,00,000

## Usage

Profit is used in:

- Financial Reporting
- Company Performance Evaluation
- Investment Decisions
- Annual Reports
- Budget Planning
- Strategic Business Decisions

---

# 3. Margin

## Definition

Margin is the percentage of revenue that remains as profit after deducting business costs.

## Business Meaning

Profit Margin measures how efficiently a company converts its sales into profit. It indicates the percentage of each sales rupee that the company keeps as earnings. Businesses use profit margin to compare products, departments, and business units. A higher margin usually indicates better cost management and greater operational efficiency.

## Example

Revenue = ₹1,00,000

Profit = ₹20,000

Profit Margin = (₹20,000 ÷ ₹1,00,000) × 100

Profit Margin = 20%

## Usage

Margin is used in:

- Profitability Analysis
- Product Comparison
- Pricing Strategy
- Financial Planning
- Business Performance Reports

---

# 4. COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)

## Definition

Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) represents the direct costs incurred in producing or purchasing the products that a company sells.

## Business Meaning

COGS includes expenses directly related to manufacturing or acquiring products, such as raw materials, factory labor, and manufacturing overhead. It excludes indirect expenses like marketing, office rent, and administrative salaries. Businesses calculate COGS to determine Gross Profit and evaluate production efficiency. Controlling COGS helps improve profitability and pricing decisions.

## Example

Raw Materials = ₹2,00,000

Direct Labor = ₹1,00,000

Manufacturing Cost = ₹50,000

COGS = ₹3,50,000

## Usage

COGS is used in:

- Gross Profit Calculation
- Financial Statements
- Inventory Valuation
- Cost Analysis
- Pricing Decisions

---

# 5. Discount

## Definition

A discount is a reduction in the original selling price offered to customers.

## Business Meaning

Businesses provide discounts to increase sales volume, attract new customers, promote products, and clear excess inventory. While discounts reduce the selling price of individual products, they often increase total sales by encouraging more purchases. Companies carefully balance discounts to maximize revenue without significantly reducing profitability.

## Example

Original Price = ₹2,000

Discount = 15%

Selling Price = ₹1,700

## Usage

Discount is used in:

- Promotional Campaigns
- Festival Sales
- Marketing Strategies
- Customer Loyalty Programs
- Sales Reports

---

# 6. Customer

## Definition

A customer is an individual or organization that purchases products or services from a business.

## Business Meaning

Customers are the primary source of business revenue. Organizations analyze customer behavior, purchasing habits, preferences, demographics, and buying frequency to improve products, enhance customer satisfaction, and develop targeted marketing campaigns. Maintaining strong customer relationships contributes to long-term business success.

## Example

A customer purchases a smartphone from an online shopping website.

The buyer becomes a customer of that company.

## Usage

Customer information is used in:

- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Customer Segmentation
- Marketing Campaigns
- Sales Analysis
- Customer Support
- Personalized Recommendations

---

# 7. Sales

## Definition

Sales refer to the quantity or value of products and services sold during a specific period.

## Business Meaning

Sales represent the actual business transactions completed with customers. Strong sales generally indicate high customer demand and effective marketing strategies. Businesses monitor sales continuously to measure employee performance, product popularity, market demand, and revenue generation.

## Example

A company sells 300 smartphones during one month.

Monthly Sales = 300 Units

## Usage

Sales information is used in:

- Revenue Analysis
- Sales Dashboards
- Performance Reports
- Business Forecasting
- Target Monitoring

---

# 8. Inventory

## Definition

Inventory refers to the stock of products, raw materials, and finished goods available for sale or production.

## Business Meaning

Inventory is one of the most valuable assets of a business. Proper inventory management ensures that products are available when customers need them while minimizing storage costs and preventing stock shortages. Efficient inventory control improves customer satisfaction and overall operational efficiency.

## Example

Warehouse Inventory:

- 500 Laptops
- 700 Keyboards
- 300 Monitors

These products together represent the company's inventory.

## Usage

Inventory is used in:

- Warehouse Management
- Supply Chain Management
- Stock Monitoring
- Production Planning
- Purchasing Decisions
- Inventory Forecasting

---

# 9. Forecast

## Definition

Forecast is the process of predicting future business performance using historical data, market trends, and analytical techniques.

## Business Meaning

Forecasting enables organizations to anticipate future sales, revenue, customer demand, production requirements, and inventory needs. Businesses use forecasting to reduce uncertainty, optimize resource allocation, and support strategic decision-making. Accurate forecasting improves operational planning and reduces business risks.

## Example

Historical Sales:

January = 900 Units

February = 1,000 Units

March = 1,100 Units

Forecasted Sales for April = 1,150 Units

## Usage

Forecasting is used in:

- Demand Planning
- Budget Preparation
- Inventory Planning
- Capacity Planning
- Business Strategy
- Financial Forecasting

---

# 10. Growth

## Definition

Growth refers to the increase in business performance over a period of time, such as revenue, profit, sales, or customer base.

## Business Meaning

Business growth indicates that an organization is expanding successfully and improving its overall performance. Companies regularly measure growth to evaluate strategic initiatives, compare current performance with previous periods, identify new opportunities, and support future planning. Sustainable growth is considered one of the primary goals of every business.

## Example

Revenue in 2024 = ₹40,00,000

Revenue in 2025 = ₹50,00,000

Growth = ((₹50,00,000 − ₹40,00,000) ÷ ₹40,00,000) × 100

Growth = 25%

## Usage

Growth is used in:

- Business Performance Measurement
- Executive Dashboards
- Strategic Planning
- Investor Reports
- Market Analysis
- Business Expansion Planning

# Conclusion

A Business Glossary establishes a common understanding of essential business terminology across an organization. Standardized definitions improve collaboration between business users and technical teams, reduce communication gaps, and ensure consistency in Business Intelligence reports, Semantic Layers, dashboards, and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). Maintaining a comprehensive business glossary enhances data quality, supports informed decision-making, and contributes to more reliable business analysis.
