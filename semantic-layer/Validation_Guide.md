
# Validation Rules

The Semantic Layer validates business data before analytics.

## Revenue

- Must be numeric
- Cannot be negative

---

## Profit

- Numeric
- May be negative

---

## Orders

- Integer
- Cannot be negative

---

## Customers

- Integer
- Required

---

## Quantity

- Integer
- Positive

---

## Discount

- Between 0 and 100

---

## Profit Margin

- Between 0 and 100

---

## Date

- Valid date

---

## Region

- Cannot be empty

---

## Category

- Cannot be empty
